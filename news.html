Recognition

Honors

2024

Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE)

Dr. Chen was selected by President Biden for the PECASE — billed by the White House as the highest honor bestowed by the U.S. government on outstanding early-career scientists and engineers. VUMC Reporter coverage.

2021

Fellow of the American Medical Informatics Association (FAMIA)

Dr. Chen was inducted into the Fellows of AMIA, recognizing sustained contributions to the field of medical informatics.

2026

Top-viewed article in Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics

"Discovering Severe Adverse Reactions From Pharmacokinetic Drug-Drug Interactions Through Literature Analysis and Electronic Health Record Verification" was recognized as a top-viewed article.

2022

Outstanding Researcher Award

Department of Biomedical Informatics, Vanderbilt University Medical Center. Dr. Chen also received an editorial-excellence award as Associate Editor of Smart Health (2020).
