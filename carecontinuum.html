CareContinuum

Our iOS app that helps patients stay on top of obesity and weight-management care in one place — not scattered across notes and portals.

iOS App · Health & Fitness

Continuous care, in your pocket

CareContinuum was developed by the OHPEN Lab as part of our research on identifying and mitigating gaps in continuous obesity care, a human-AI collaborative project leveraging longitudinal EHR data. It brings together health tracking, care organization, and AI-supported insights for adults working with a care team on overweight or obesity — including those in research or specialty programs where the app is offered.

Download on the App Store Visit carecontinuum.ai

Features

What you can do

📈 Track health metrics

Log and review weight, blood pressure, activity, and other health metrics over time to see the full picture of your care journey.

🗂️ Stay organized

Keep medications, appointments, and care-related documents organized in one place instead of scattered across notes and portals.

🤖 AI-supported insights

Explore insights that highlight barriers, practical steps, and priority actions — grounded in the data you choose to share.

🔗 Trusted next steps

Follow clear links to trusted care resources when your analysis suggests next steps, such as program information.

Good to know

Important information

Not a substitute for your clinician

CareContinuum does not diagnose conditions or replace your clinician, and it does not provide emergency care. Use the app together with your licensed healthcare provider, and call emergency services for urgent symptoms.

Privacy & research

See the Privacy Policy at carecontinuum.ai for how data is handled. Participation in research, where applicable, follows the consent you were given. For support, use the contact options published for your program or study.
